Huffstorm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Huffstorm ("the game") is made by Emilio Shahaj ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, and why. The game has no accounts and asks for no names, emails or passwords.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (gold, upgrades, villages, settings and purchases) is saved on your device. If you delete the game, this data is deleted too.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. There are no forced ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate your general location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings. On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission to track before using your device's advertising identifier. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as gems, the starter pack and the VIP pass) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. We receive a confirmation of the purchase so the game can deliver what you bought. Purchases are handled through Unity In-App Purchasing, which may collect limited technical data as described in Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Clock check

When the game starts, it makes a small request to a Google server to read the current time, so daily rewards and timers work fairly even if the device clock is changed. This request does not include any personal information; like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to that server.

Game engine and crash data

The game is built with Unity. Unity may collect basic, non-identifying technical data (device model, operating system, performance and crash information) to keep the game working well.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in the game's Settings (where required by law).
  • Reset or limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the game, or with "Wipe save" in Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
